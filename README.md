# SuperStore Sales Performance & Multi-Page BI Analytics Dashboard

**Role & Execution:** BI & Data Analyst.

- **System Scope:** Executed an end-to-end retail sales analysis across three weekly phases (data preprocessing, decision-maker analytical questioning, and interactive dashboarding) to evaluate revenue growth, customer repeat behavior, regional and category performance, and product revenue concentration.

- **Technical Implementation:** Cleaned and prepared the dataset in Power BI, formulated targeted business intelligence questions, and engineered a 3-page interactive dashboard (Executive Overview, Sales Analysis, and Product Analysis) featuring dynamic slicers (Year, Region, Category, Segment), core KPIs (Total Sales, Repeat Rate %, Total Orders, Total Customers, Avg Order Value, YoY Sales Growth, Sales YTD, Top 10% Revenue Share), monthly/quarterly/day-of-week trends, regional and segment breakdowns, and top-product ranking visuals.

- **Tech Stack:** Microsoft Power BI, Data Preprocessing, DAX & KPI Engineering, Data Visualization, Interactive Slicers, Exploratory Data Analysis.
